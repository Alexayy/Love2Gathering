## Owner-supplied material and removal requests

At the project owner's explicit request, Beleren and the Classic Magic /
Mystic Eye backs remain in private builds and installers shared with friends.
This records the owner's intended use, not permission from the asset owners.
Attribution, private hosting, free access and a removal promise do not supply
missing redistribution rights.

An asset owner can contact the [project maintainer](https://github.com/Alexayy)
through the repository where accessible, or the person who supplied a copy.
Identify the asset and the relevant rights. The maintainer will review the
request and remove identified material from future distributions on a
substantiated rights-holder request. Already distributed copies cannot be
recalled automatically.
