# User-supplied card backs

`classic-magic.jpg` and `mystic-eye.png` are unmodified copies of the two images supplied by the project owner on 2026-11-14. They appear as **Classic Magic** and **Mystic Eye** under Settings → Presentation → Card back.

Both render upright, without color tint or Kenney overlays. Their complete image fits the card-back rectangle with aspect ratio preserved. Original files remain untouched; hashes and dimensions are recorded in `provenance.json`.

No separate redistribution license or artist attribution accompanied these attachments. They are not included under the Kenney CC0 notices. The Classic Magic image displays Magic: The Gathering branding; no original authorship is claimed by this project.
